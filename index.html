La Nostra Storia

Italian Soul, Cape Verdean Warmth

Founded in 2015 by Chef Matteo Ricci, Osteria n.3 brings the authentic flavors of Tuscany to the shores of Santiago. We use only the finest ingredients – many imported directly from Italy – combined with local seafood and produce.

Every dish is made with passion, from our slow‑simmered ragù to our hand‑stretched pizza dough. We invite you to experience a taste of Italy in an elegant yet relaxed setting.
